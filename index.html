    📋 How to Submit Your Report — Step by Step

    🌐 Option 1: Online Portal (Easiest)

    1. Open complaint.nccia.gov.pk
    2. Click "File a Complaint"
    3. Enter your name, CNIC number, and phone number
    4. Copy the draft above and paste it in the complaint box
    5. Upload any screenshots or evidence you have
    6. Submit — you will receive an official case number — keep it safe

    ⏱️ Response time: Usually 24–48 hours

    📞 Option 2: Helpline Call

    1. Call 1799 (NCCIA helpline — free)
    2. Say: "I want to report a cybercrime"
    3. They will register the case and give you a complaint number

    🕐 Monday–Saturday, 9 AM – 5 PM

    🏢 Option 3: Physical Office

    1. Visit your nearest NCCIA / FIA office
    2. Print this draft and bring it
    3. Bring original CNIC and a photocopy
    4. Bring printed evidence (screenshots, transaction records)

    ⚠️ Important — Please Read

    • Filing a complaint is completely free
    • No real officer will ask for money on WhatsApp or call — that is fraud
    • You cannot be refused an FIR — it is your legal right
    • Report as soon as possible — do not delete any evidence
    • After filing, save the official case number for tracking

    Your Rights

    • Filing a complaint is free — no fee is charged.
    • You cannot be refused the right to file an FIR/complaint.
    • Real officers never send an "arrest warrant" over WhatsApp or demand money on a video call.
    • You can ask for your complaint's reference number and follow up on its status.
    • Providing evidence isn't mandatory but speeds up the investigation.
